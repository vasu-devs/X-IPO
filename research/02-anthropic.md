## 2. Revenue (verified 3-0)
- Run-rate revenue **crossed $47B in May 2026** (company-disclosed annualized run-rate, not audited GAAP), driven by enterprise Claude adoption for **coding and agentic workflows**.
- Trajectory context: ~$1B run-rate (early 2025) → ~$5B (mid-2025) → ~$9B (end-2025, reported) → $47B (May 2026). This is the steepest revenue ramp ever recorded at this scale.
- Implied multiple at the $965B mark: ~**20.5x** run-rate revenue — *below* OpenAI's ~36x and below the comparables model's 25x danger line. On multiple compression risk, Anthropic prices like the "growth-justifies-it" case.

## 3. IPO signals (verified 3-0)
- **Confidential draft S-1 submitted June 1, 2026**, days after the Series H closed — formal start of the IPO process.
- No share count, price, ticker, exchange, or firm date; offering explicitly conditional on market conditions.
- Anthropic is a Public Benefit Corporation with a Long-Term Benefit Trust — governance structure will be a disclosure focus, similar to OpenAI's nonprofit-controlled PBC.

## 4. Model-relevant notes
- The Series H investor set (Altimeter, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, Sequoia) is dominated by crossover/public-market funds — the classic "pre-IPO pricing committee" pattern that historically compresses the IPO pop (the last private round is effectively the price discovery).
- Anthropic enters the window with the **best multiple-to-growth ratio** of the three AI candidates; the main risks are run-rate durability (one-month annualization), compute-cost gross margins, and the sheer absolute size of any float.
