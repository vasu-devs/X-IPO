## Model outputs (probability-weighted bands; methodology in 07)

### SpaceXAI (SPCX) — prices tonight at fixed $135, lists Jun 12
- Day-1 return: **P10 -8% · P50 +22% · P90 +65%** (fixed price + ~4.3% float + retail demand vs a twitchy tape)

- Why: Aramco-pattern small float supports price; fixed-price discovery widens day-1 variance; lockup supply and Nasdaq-100 inclusion fight it out in months 6-12.

### OpenAI
- Listing window: **~12% by Sep 2026 · ~48% by Dec 2026** (Polymarket, Jun 11) · model: ~80% by end-2027
- Pricing valuation: **P10 $700B · P50 $950B · P90 $1.15T**

### Anthropic
- Listing window: model: **~40% in 2026, ~85% by end-2027** (no public prediction market; S-1 filed)
- Pricing valuation: **P10 $900B · P50 $1.15T · P90 $1.5T**
- Day-1: **P10 0% · P50 +24% · P90 +55%**
- 12-month vs offer: **P10 -30% · P50 +20% · P90 +95%**
- Why: best multiple-to-growth cover (20x on $47B growing); risk concentrates in run-rate durability and float size.

## The single biggest conditioning event
SPCX's first month of trading. A strong debut likely pulls OpenAI and Anthropic into Q4 2026 windows; a broken one into a >10% Nasdaq drawdown pushes both to 2027 and compresses every band above.

## Honest caveats
- All revenue figures are company-disclosed or leaked, unaudited; run-rates annualize single months.
- Private marks re-rated so fast in H1 2026 that several "current" figures were refuted as stale within weeks; these may be too.
- N=20 comparables anchor the day-1 model; an $852B-1.75T listing is out-of-sample by an order of magnitude.
- This is a research exercise, not investment advice.
