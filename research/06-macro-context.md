# Macro Context — The Tape These IPOs Land On (June 2026)

> Compiled 2026-06-11.

## 1. The June 2026 tape (sourced: CNN, TheStreet, GuruFocus)
- **Record highs on June 2, 2026**, then a sharp AI-led selloff: by June 9 the S&P 500 was down ~3% and the Nasdaq ~5% from those highs.
- **June 5**: Nasdaq-100 fell **4.77%**, Nasdaq Composite **4.18%** — a ~$1T single-day wipeout led by semiconductors, triggered by strong payrolls (rate-cut hopes dented), a skeptical AI research report, and a Meta secondary offering.
- June 9: dip-buying stabilized the tape (Nasdaq -0.97%, S&P -0.26%).
- YTD: S&P +~8%, Nasdaq +~10% — still a constructive year despite the wobble.
- **Rates: 10-year Treasury yield above 4.5%** — a persistent valuation headwind for long-duration growth assets.

## 2. The AI bubble debate (GMO viewpoint, survived 2-1 verification)
- GMO (Jan 2026): total AI industry revenue **under $50B against ~$1T+ of investment** — the core bear exhibit.
- Counterpoint that emerged after GMO published: Anthropic's $47B run-rate alone (May 2026) nearly equals GMO's whole-industry revenue estimate, and OpenAI runs ~$24B annualized. Either the revenue gap is closing at historic speed, or run-rate accounting is flattering the picture. Both can be true.
- "Circular deals" critique (Bloomberg): NVIDIA/Amazon/Microsoft equity stakes intertwined with compute purchase commitments raise revenue-quality questions that S-1 scrutiny will force into the open.

## 3. The IPO window
- 2025 reopened the tech IPO window (CoreWeave, Circle, Chime, Figma, Klarna); 2026 escalated it to the mega-cap era: **SpaceXAI prices the largest IPO in history ($75B at up to $1.75T) on June 12, 2026.**
- Cleary Gottlieb's 2026 outlook: global IPO volumes recovering, with AI listings the dominant theme.
- The window's fragility is live: the June 5 drawdown happened *during* the SPCX roadshow. The comparables model treats a >15% Nasdaq drawdown as window-closing; at ~5% off highs, the window is open but twitchy.

## 4. Sequencing — the macro variable nobody can diversify
1. **June 12, 2026: SPCX lists.** First $1T-class AI-adjacent debut. Day-1 and month-1 performance becomes the de facto price discovery for the entire private AI complex.
2. **H2 2026: OpenAI (S-1 in) and Anthropic (S-1 in) choose windows.** Polymarket says Sept is unlikely (~12%), year-end is a coin flip (~48%).
3. Reflexivity: these deals are large enough to *move* the macro tape they depend on — $75B+ of new supply absorbs liquidity; index inclusion later forces passive buying.
