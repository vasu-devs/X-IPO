# OpenAI (ChatGPT) — IPO Research Dossier

> Compiled 2026-06-11. All claims below passed 3-vote adversarial verification (vote tallies noted) unless flagged.
> Status: **Confidential draft S-1 submitted to the SEC, announced June 8, 2026.**

## 1. Valuation history
| Date | Event | Valuation (post-money) |
|---|---|---|
| Jan 2023 | Microsoft expanded partnership era | ~$30B (refuted as "current" by verifiers; historical only) |
| Oct 2025 | Secondary / employee sale marks | ~$500B reported |
| **Mar 31, 2026** | **$122B funding round closes (largest private raise in history)** | **$852B** |
| Sep 2026? | Reported IPO target (Fortune/WSJ) | up to **$1,000B** |

Verified 3-0: the $122B round at $852B post-money, co-led by SoftBank with a16z, D. E. Shaw Ventures, MGX, TPG, and T. Rowe Price; anchored strategically by Amazon, NVIDIA, SoftBank; Microsoft participating; crossover/institutional names include Fidelity, BlackRock affiliates, Sequoia, Thrive, Temasek, UC Investments, ARK. Over **$3B came from retail investors via bank channels**, and OpenAI was included in ARK Invest ETFs while still private. CFO Sarah Friar: the valuation would rank among the 15 largest S&P 500 companies.

A stale claim that OpenAI was "recently valued at $750B" was **refuted 0-3** (superseded by the $852B round).

## 2. Revenue & profitability (verified 3-0 except where noted)
- End-2024: ~$1B per quarter.
- FY2025: **$13.1B revenue**; operating loss ~**$8B** (GMO estimate, 2-1 vote, treat as approximate). CFO later cited a ~$20B ARR exit rate for end-2025.
- Q1 2026: ~**$5.7-6B**, boosted by Codex adoption.
- As of March 2026: **~$2B revenue per month** (~$24B annualized).
- Still unprofitable and cash-burning. (A claim that OpenAI "has not publicly disclosed revenue" was refuted 0-3 — it has.)
- Pre-IPO cost discipline: **Sora short-form video app shut down April 26, 2026**, framed by CNBC as cost-cutting ahead of the IPO.

## 3. IPO signals (verified 3-0)
- Confidential draft S-1 submitted to the SEC; **publicly announced June 8, 2026** (reportedly prepared/filed ~May 22; a claim pinning the filing to June 9 was refuted 0-3).
- Fortune/WSJ reporting: listing **as soon as September 2026 at up to $1T**. OpenAI's own statement: "We have not decided on timing yet"; not imminent.
- **Polymarket (fetched live June 11, 2026):** P(listed) <1% by Jun 30 · ~4% by Aug 31 · ~12% by Sep 30 · **~48% by Dec 31, 2026** (Dec market volume ~$673K — modest liquidity). A 2026 listing is roughly a coin flip; a September debut is priced as unlikely despite the S-1.
- No share count, price, ticker, or exchange set.

## 4. Structure & governance considerations
- OpenAI Group PBC under nonprofit OpenAI Foundation control (Oct 2025 restructuring); Microsoft holds a large minority stake. Governance discount risk vs clean single-class peers (see comparables file §2.6).
- Implied multiple at a $1T listing: ~**42x** annualized revenue (vs ~36x at the $852B mark) — far above the ~25x danger threshold in the comparables model, mitigated only if growth stays >80% y/y (current trajectory: FY25 $13.1B → ~$24B run-rate = ~85-90% y/y).
