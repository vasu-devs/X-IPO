# Investor Positioning — How Capital Is Lining Up (June 2026)

> Compiled 2026-06-11. Verified findings noted; the rest is sourced press reporting.

## 1. The cap tables already look like public registers
The defining feature of this cycle: by the time these companies file, the public-market buyer base is **already inside**.

### OpenAI's $122B round (verified 3-0) included:
- **VC / growth**: SoftBank (co-lead), a16z, D. E. Shaw Ventures, MGX, TPG, Sequoia, Thrive
- **Mutual funds / crossover**: T. Rowe Price (co-lead), Fidelity, BlackRock affiliates
- **Sovereign / institutional**: MGX (Abu Dhabi), Temasek, UC Investments
- **Strategic**: Amazon, NVIDIA, Microsoft
- **Retail**: **over $3B via bank distribution channels** — retail in a private mega-round is unprecedented at this scale
- **ETF inclusion while private**: OpenAI added to ARK Invest ETFs

### Anthropic's $65B Series H (verified 3-0):
- Led by **Altimeter, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, Sequoia** — predominantly crossover funds that also run public books. The last private round is functionally the IPO pricing committee.

### SpaceXAI:
- NVIDIA and Cisco-linked investors in xAI's Jan 2026 $20B round; the June 12 IPO book reportedly anchored by index-scale long-onlys given the fixed $135 price.

## 2. What each investor class is doing
| Class | Behavior in this cycle | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| VCs (a16z, Sequoia, Thrive) | Doubling down at $850B-965B marks rather than distributing | Conviction, but also lock-in: exits now require public markets |
| Mutual funds (Fidelity, T. Rowe, BlackRock) | Buying pre-IPO at scale; marks now anchor IPO pricing | Demand pull-forward; smaller pop, deeper book |
| Sovereign wealth (MGX, Temasek, PIF-adjacent) | Anchoring mega-rounds; patient capital | Floor under valuations; geopolitical scrutiny risk |
| Hedge funds / crossovers (Altimeter, Dragoneer, Greenoaks) | Led Anthropic H; classic pre-IPO positioning | They price the deal before bankers do |
| Retail | $3B+ into OpenAI via bank channels; ARK ETF exposure; Polymarket speculation on timing | Retail demand exists pre-listing — supports day-1 but raises froth risk |
| Strategics (NVIDIA, Amazon, Microsoft) | Equity stakes tied to compute/commercial deals | "Circular deal" criticism (Bloomberg graphics piece); revenue-quality questions in S-1 review |

## 3. Secondary / tender context
- OpenAI: Oct 2025 employee secondary at ~$500B → $852B primary five months later (+70%); pre-IPO platforms (Forge etc.) quote OpenAI actively (unverified pricing).
- Anthropic: tender-offer reporting at ~$350B in early 2026 → $965B Series H (+176% in months). Bloomberg reported an April 2026 tender where employees largely held rather than sold — insiders expecting more upside.
- The velocity of mark re-rating (claims of $750B/OpenAI and $350B/Anthropic were *refuted as stale* by verifiers within weeks) is itself a key datum: **private marks are moving faster than the verification cycle**.

## 4. Demand-model implication
Book demand for any of these listings is effectively pre-validated: the marginal IPO buyer (long-only mutual fund, sovereign, retail) already owns or bid in the last private round. The model therefore assumes **10-20x+ oversubscription, small floats, and modest engineered pops** rather than Figma-style 250% explosions.
