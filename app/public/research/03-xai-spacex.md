# xAI / X Corp → SpaceXAI (SPCX) — IPO Research Dossier

> Compiled 2026-06-11. Sourced via targeted search (CNBC, Fortune, Reuters-cited reporting); the xAI leg did not pass the
> full 3-vote adversarial pipeline, so treat figures as well-sourced press reporting rather than verified claims.
> Status: **Going public TOMORROW — June 12, 2026, Nasdaq, ticker SPCX, inside SpaceX.**

## 1. The structural surprise: there is no standalone xAI IPO
The xAI path diverged completely from OpenAI/Anthropic:
- **Mar 2025**: xAI ($80B) merged with X Corp ($33B) into XAI Holdings (~$113B).
- **Jan 2026**: xAI raised **$20B at a reported $230B valuation** (investors incl. NVIDIA, Cisco-linked funds) — one of the largest private AI rounds ever.
- **Feb 2026**: **SpaceX acquired xAI in an all-stock transaction valuing xAI at ~$250B** — described as the largest private corporate merger in history — creating a combined entity at **$1.25T**; rebranded **SpaceXAI** in May 2026.
- **Apr 1, 2026**: SpaceX confidentially filed for IPO. Public S-1 May 20, 2026; Amendment No. 1 June 1, 2026 (reg. no. 333-296070).
- **Jun 3, 2026**: Fixed IPO price of **$135/share**, **555.6M shares**, ~**$75B raise** — the largest IPO in history — at a valuation reported up to **$1.75T**.
- **Jun 12, 2026**: Target Nasdaq listing date, ticker **SPCX**.

## 2. xAI fundamentals inside the deal
- Q3 2025 (quarter ended Sep 30): xAI revenue **$107M**, net loss **$1.46B** (Reuters-reported).
- End-2025: ~**$3.83B annualized revenue on a combined xAI + X basis** (Sacra estimate); xAI standalone ~$500M annualized.
- Grok: U.S. chatbot market share **17.8% in Jan 2026** (vs 1.9% a year earlier); **~117M MAU by Mar 2026** per the SpaceX IPO filing (35M Dec 2025).
- The xAI acquisition pushed consolidated SpaceX to a reported **$5B loss for 2025** and added ~**$1B/month cash burn**.

## 3. Model-relevant notes
- SPCX is a **hybrid asset**: Starlink/launch cash flows + an AI lab + X's social platform. The AI exposure is real but diluted; it prices partly as aerospace infrastructure, partly as AI scarcity.
- **Low free float** (~4.3% at $1.75T) — the Aramco pattern: mechanically supported price, forced index-inclusion buying later, high volatility on thin supply.
- The **fixed $135 price** (no bookbuild range) is unusual and Musk-typical; it transfers price discovery entirely to day-1 trading, widening the plausible day-1 distribution in both directions.
- SPCX day-1 is the **single most important conditioning event** for OpenAI's and Anthropic's IPO windows: a strong debut likely pulls both listings into Q4 2026; a broken one (especially into the early-June Nasdaq drawdown) pushes them to 2027.
